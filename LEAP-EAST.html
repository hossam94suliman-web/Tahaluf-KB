    Participant Details:

    Name:
    Passport Number:
    Expiry Date:
    Date of Arrival:
    Date of Departure:

     After the exhibitor fills those details and sends it to us, then we will fill that Microsoft Word document with those details and convert it into PDF while we save it, and adding password to it which is standard (Company name+ Event year), then send it to the exhibitor all the PDFs and sending the password in another email which includes only the exhibitors for privacy and security purposes.

    The Exhibitor (Manual - Portal - Customer Centre) They're all the same thing. 😁

    It's the place where they could nominate their contractor from (if they're exhibiting with space only stand). Also it contains the manuals and form for any service they might need. Moreover it has the deadlines and sometimes it contains the exhibitor marketing materials (banners and logos that they are exhibiting in (name of event)) so they could share it on all social media and LinkedIn.
